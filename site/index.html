Listening study

Thank you for helping. This takes about 25–30 minutes and you do it once.

What you will do

You will hear short recordings of a synthetic voice. Every recording speaks the same neutral sentences, so the only thing that differs is the voice.

  • Rate how natural the voice sounds, on a five-point scale.
  • Mark what emotion the speaker was expressing, by clicking one point on a square.

There are no right answers you are expected to know. Guessing when unsure is part of the design — the study measures how clearly the voice communicates, not how well you perform.

You need

  • Headphones or earphones — laptop speakers flatten exactly what is being measured.
  • A quiet room and about 30 uninterrupted minutes.
  • A computer, not a phone.
  • To set the volume once at the start and not change it. Loudness is one of the things under test.

Your data

Only your answers are recorded — no name, no email, nothing that identifies you. Nothing is sent anywhere: at the end your answers stay in your browser and you copy or download them and send them back yourself. There is no server and no third party involved.

You can stop at any point by closing the tab. Partial answers are not used.
