HOW VOICES EXPRESS EMOTION

Different engines.
Different controls.

Kokoro

Speed and acoustic controls

A fixed learned voice with native speed control, calibrated level and external pitch shifting. There is no native emotion-label input.

Engine source ↗
Chatterbox · StyleTTS2 · ZipVoice

Reference-conditioned voices

Emotion-bearing reference recordings influence the generated voice. Chatterbox also controls expressiveness; StyleTTS2 mixes a reference style with a diffusion-generated style.

Chatterbox source ↗
CosyVoice2 · Parler-TTS

Describe the delivery

Text instructions describe the chosen emotion. CosyVoice2 keeps a neutral speaker reference fixed; Parler-TTS uses a fixed speaker cue. Listener ratings are needed to establish emotional coherence.

THE RESEARCH QUESTION

Does the voice express
the emotion you selected?

ECHO studies the relationship between a user-defined emotional target and the emotion listeners hear. Comparing controlled settings helps investigate which parameters affect each target, how effects differ between engines, and what naturalness costs they carry.
