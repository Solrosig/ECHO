Before you begin

You will hear 45 short clips from nine systems, allow roughly 20–30 minutes, including breaks; actual time varies. Voice and system names are hidden. First rate the emotion and naturalness you hear; then compare the voice with its intended emotion.

Use headphones in a quiet place. Set a comfortable volume before starting and keep it unchanged. Play each clip at normal speed. Please complete this test before trying Test mode or Explore. Take a short break after clips 15 and 30. Read the step-by-step listener instructions.

Taking part is voluntary. We ask for a nickname, not your real name or email. Your nickname, ratings, playback information and random participant code are sent to the study database as you continue, including an unfinished session. A browser backup lets you retry if your connection drops. The researcher uses these records for the thesis study. You can stop at any time and use Delete my ratings on this device to remove your stored ratings. Downloading a CSV is optional.

Use the same nickname throughout your participation. A private random participant code distinguishes sessions with matching nicknames.
