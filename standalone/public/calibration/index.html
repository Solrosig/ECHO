Calibration gallery (retired)

This page once played eSpeak NG, Kokoro and Piper before and after the acoustic calibration of 7 September 2026. The gallery is not part of the current study, and its recordings are not published: labelled voice examples could influence the blinded listening test. Only this record of the method remains.

What the calibration changed

Each engine has its own rate and pitch settings. eSpeak uses native pitch. Kokoro and Piper use an external pitch processor that preserves duration but does not preserve formants. A common active-RMS reference precedes the emotion-specific gain.

Only mean pitch offset, timing and signal level are controlled. Voice quality and detailed intonation are not independently calibrated. Naturalness and emotional coherence await independent listeners.

Calibration: 7 September 2026.
