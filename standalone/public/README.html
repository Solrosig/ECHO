About project

What ECHO is

ECHO (Emotionally CoHerent cOnversational system) is an academic project for building a conversational system as playground to test emotional coherence in Text-to-Speech engines. The system turns English text input into spoken audio and lets you select emotional background and try conversations with a speaking assistant. The emotion is always declared by the user. The selection is made from the four quadrants of Russell's circumplex model. The system produces a reply and speaks it aloud, and the selected emotion must be present in the voice that delivers it.

The system runs entirely on open or freely available models and speech engines. No commercial voice service is used.

The research goal

Evaluate text-to-speech engines for their capacity to control emotion and for the emotional coherence of what they produce and select the best fit for the system.

The project explores whether changes to a computer-generated voice can convey an intended emotion clearly while keeping the speech natural and understandable.

Your feedback helps answer:

  • What emotion and energy do people actually hear in the voice?
  • Does the speech sound natural and remain comfortable to listen to?
  • Do the spoken voice, the written reply, and the chosen emotion fit together?
  • Can a person understand and use the prototype without getting stuck?
