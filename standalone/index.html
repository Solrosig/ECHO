A RESEARCH PROTOTYPE

How does a voice express your emotion?

ECHO explores the connection between the emotion you choose and the way text-to-speech expresses it. Compare voices, test the same words and explore a conversation — helping us understand which voice controls communicate emotion more clearly.

Choose an alias to identify your responses.

Your data, explained

We store your nickname and listening ratings after you consent. Test phrases, chat messages and voice settings require separate consent in those modes. Use an alias and avoid personal information. Your nickname is saved in this browser; no account is required.
